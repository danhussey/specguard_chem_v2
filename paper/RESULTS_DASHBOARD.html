Label Guide

QSAR models: all three train on support-set Morgan fingerprints and measured activity, then rank feasible candidates by predicted activity.

qsar_rf is a random forest regressor; qsar_gbt is a gradient-boosting regressor; qsar_svm is a sparse-scaled linear-kernel support-vector regressor.

Validator: deterministic harness checks and repair. It does not inspect hidden activity values.

Oracle: hidden-activity upper bound used only to show remaining decision headroom.

Direct JSON: final-answer JSON prompting designed to avoid visible-output budget failures.
